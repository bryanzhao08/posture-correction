This Privacy Policy explains how [Company Name] ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the FormCoach mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to processing your data transparently and securely.

## 1. What We Collect and How We Use It

### Video Processing
**We do not collect, upload, or store your video.** All camera video is processed entirely locally on your phone in real-time to analyze your motion. The raw video never leaves your device.

### Performance Data
When you complete a session, we upload the following summary data to our servers to track your progress and generate reports:
- The sport played.
- Session timestamp and duration.
- Repetition counts.
- Per-rep form metrics and scores.

### Body-Pose Keypoints (Opt-In Only)
If you explicitly opt-in via the App's settings, we upload anonymous body-pose keypoints (the mathematical coordinates of 13 body joints per frame) for your sessions. This data contains **no images or video** and is used exclusively to improve the accuracy of our motion-tracking algorithms.

### Account Data
To provide the service, we collect:
- Your name.
- Your email address.
- A securely hashed version of your password (using bcrypt).

## 2. Third-Party Processors

We do not sell your data, run ads, or use third-party analytics platforms. 

We use **Resend**, a third-party email processor, strictly to deliver session and checkpoint reports to your email address. You may opt out of receiving these emails at any time in the App's Settings.

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion

You can permanently delete your account directly from the App's Settings. Initiating an account deletion will immediately and permanently remove:
- Your account information (name, email, password hash).
- All your session and checkpoint history.
- Any stored body-pose keypoints associated with your account.

## 4. App Store Privacy Nutrition Label

To help you understand our practices at a glance, here are our answers for the App Store's Privacy label:

- **Data Used to Track You:** None.
- **Data Linked to You:**
  - **Contact Info:** Email Address, Name (used for App Functionality and Account Management).
  - **Health & Fitness:** Fitness performance metrics and scores (used for App Functionality).
- **Data Not Linked to You:**
  - **Diagnostics/Other Data:** Body-pose keypoints (only if opted-in; used for App Improvement).
